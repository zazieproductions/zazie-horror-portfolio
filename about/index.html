Zazie Kanwar-Torge, composer of dark cinematic work.

Under Zazie Productions, Zazie Kanwar-Torge develops original music for psychological horror, supernatural thrillers, folk horror, body horror, dark science fiction, experimental music, and related audiovisual projects.

Biography

The person behind the atmosphere.

Zazie writes the music, performs it, and builds the sounds. The current practice combines composition, production, multi-instrumental performance, hybrid orchestration, custom synthesis, and tactile acoustic material such as struck strings and bowed metal.

The work is composition-first: themes, motifs, beds, and tension cues shaped for the edit. The audio reel shows standalone palette; the project archive shows how the music sits alongside picture.

Practice areas

  • Horror film composition
  • Psychological horror and thriller scoring
  • Dark ambient and industrial/noise-informed music
  • Experimental sound design and score-adjacent texture
  • Short films, features, teasers, games, and audiovisual projects
