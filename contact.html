I Request a scoring quote

Only your name and email are needed to start. This site neither stores nor sends what you type — the form composes the message, and your own mail client delivers it. The privacy notice sets out exactly what is and is not collected, and the accessibility statement describes how the form behaves with a screen reader or keyboard only.

Start with your name and email. Project details are optional—fill in only what you know. Submitting prepares an email for you to review and send; this site does not store or send your details.

A feature-film client commissioning one scene/cue may start with Individual Scene Scoring. The $2,500 indicative minimum applies only to substantial feature-film or experimental-cinema scores.

Optional project details minutes, timing, budget, references

Leave anything blank if you are unsure. You do not need to share a rough cut in your first message.

Unreleased material is treated as confidential. A mutual NDA is available before anything is shared.

Independent and micro-budget productions are welcome to inquire. Where appropriate, a reduced musical scope or licensing of existing material may offer a more accessible alternative.

Payment terms: 50% deposit before work begins. Remaining 50% due before release of final approved deliverables, subject to the written agreement. Applies to new commissions only; existing contracts are unchanged.

II What to send

For a more specific quote, add any details you already have. You can leave unknowns blank and follow up later.

  1. The format: short film, feature, series episode, game, trailer or installation.
  2. The scored minutes of music you need, and the cue count if you know it.
  3. The delivery date or festival deadline.
  4. A budget range, if you are comfortable sharing one. It can help tailor scope; “not sure yet” is fine.
  5. Two or three reference films or tracks for tone, and what each one does for the scene.
  6. A private link to a rough cut, if available. It helps refine the final scope; a scene list is enough to start, and the cut can follow later.

Unreleased material is treated as confidential; tell me if you need a non-disclosure agreement before you send anything.

After you send it

You receive a written, nonbinding quote that sets out the scope, the revision rounds and the licence. Nothing is booked until scope, licence and payment terms are agreed in writing. See the FAQ on what a commission costs, what moves the price and who owns the music.
