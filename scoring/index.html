Original music written to picture

Horror film scoring with a point of view.

Zazie Kanwar-Torge composes dark, atmospheric music for horror films, thrillers, short-form work, and audiovisual projects. The score is built around the cut: tension, silence, motif, texture, and the exact moment a scene changes temperature.

Hear the scoring reelDiscuss a project

What the work covers

A bespoke score, not a library substitute.

The current portfolio centers on original composition: themes, beds, stingers, and atmosphere shaped for picture. Film samples include sleep-paralysis horror, short horror films, supernatural material, thriller sequences, and dark cinematic cues.

Tension and dread

Psychological pressure

Slow-burn harmony, unstable pulses, negative space, and motifs that can stay under dialogue without flattening a scene.

Picture-led writing

Music that follows the edit

Spotting starts with story function: where music enters, where it leaves, what a reveal needs, and what the audience should feel before they can name it.

Delivery

Usable production assets

Project scope can include original cues, stems, alternate mixes, tension variants, and a picture-locked handoff. The exact delivery list is agreed before work begins.

A focused horror practice

From the first cue to the final handoff.

Composition is the center of the service. Sound design can be discussed when it belongs to the project brief, but this is not a claim to provide production sound, foley, or a full post-production department.

For independent filmmakers

Shorts, features, teasers, and proof-of-concept work can be scoped around the material that matters most: runtime, locked picture, story tone, references, and delivery deadlines.
