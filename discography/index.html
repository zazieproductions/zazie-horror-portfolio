IThe records, 2019 to 2026

Eighteen releases on Bandcamp, mirrored to Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer. This is not a separate body of work from the film scores - it is the same instruments, the same room and the same hands, and it is where most of the horror cues are tested before they are written to picture.

IIWhere to listen - and why the credits disagree

The same eighteen records exist on five services, and the metadata does not match between them. The writer is filed as Zazie Kanwar-Torge on Apple and Spotify, as Zazie Kanwar- Torge on Deezer because the distributor inserted a space, and as Zazie Diya Kanwar-Torge on one track of Vermiform. All three are the same composer, the same performing rights organisation and the same royalty stream.
