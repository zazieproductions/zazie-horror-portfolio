Scoring process

From inquiry to final score

A focused workflow for screen and game projects. Scope, schedule, delivery and applicable rights are confirmed in writing for each commission.

  • Collaboration Remote or in person by arrangement
  • Project terms Confirmed in writing

Stages

  1. I Inquiry
  2. II Scope and quote
  3. III Spotting
  4. IV Composition and review
  5. V Delivery

I Start with the project

Share a short description, format, approximate scored minutes, target date and any musical references you already have. A rough cut is helpful but not required for an initial conversation. The inquiry form opens a message in your email application.

II Agree the scope

After reviewing the available material, the studio sends a written quote covering the project scope, fee, schedule, revision allowance and deliverables. The quote and any signed project agreement set the applicable rights and usage terms. Work is scheduled once the written terms and payment arrangements are accepted.

III Spot the picture

The director and composer review the cut together, mark where cues begin and end, and agree each cue’s dramatic role and relationship to dialogue and sound design. The resulting cue list and shared musical direction guide the score.

IV Compose and review

Original music is written to the agreed brief and picture. Review points and the included consolidated revision rounds are set in the quote. New cues, substantial changes to the picture or requests outside the agreed scope may affect the fee and schedule; any change is discussed before work proceeds.

V Deliver the agreed files

Files are delivered electronically in the formats listed in the quote; stereo WAVs are common. Stems, alternate mixes, surround or immersive formats, live players and separate sound-design work are scoped individually. Payment, credit and permitted use are governed by the accepted written project documents.
