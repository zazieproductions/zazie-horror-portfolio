IInquiry and rough cut review

Send an email to zaziediya@gmail.com with five things: format (short, feature, series, game, trailer), scored minutes of music, delivery date or festival deadline, budget range, and two or three reference tracks or films for tone. A rough cut or assembly is useful if you have one. The studio watches it before quoting, because runtime alone never tells truth about how much music a film needs. A written quote follows review of your cut: a preliminary estimate — final quotation subject to creative scope and written agreement. Nothing is booked until the scope, licence and payment terms are agreed in writing. See how to start a commission FAQ and scoring rates.

IISpotting session - cheapest place to change mind

Cut plays head to tail with director, every cue marked: where music starts, where it stops, what it does dramatically, where it must stay out of dialogue or sound design. Output is cue sheet with timings, tone agreement per cue, delivery schedule. Spotting is cheapest place to change mind: costs nothing there and a lot later. Remote by default over video call with cut playing in sync. See spotting FAQ.

IIIComposition - psychological architecture and dark atmospheric color

Original scores for psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, cosmic horror, techno thrillers and dark science fiction. Palette is struck strings, bowed metal, hybrid orchestration and custom synthesiser builds, written to picture rather than pulled from library. Every commissioned cue is written for your picture, no stock, no generative model. See 32 original horror cues and selected horror productions.

  • 01

    Slow-burn beds, dissociative harmony, tension shaped to cut. Built for psychological horror, folk horror, supernatural thrillers. Themes that carry through film, trailer, festival cut.

  • 02

    Dark atmospheric color

    Hybrid orchestration, sub-frequency weight, frozen ambient space. Bowed metal, waterphone, custom synths. Atmospheric horror beds and dread sound design.

  • 03

    Cinematic themes and motifs

    Motifs designed to carry emotional weight of cut. Not generic. Score feels inseparable from cut: quiet dread, clear themes, nothing generic - per collaborator review.

IVRevisions - consolidated rounds by package

Individual Scene and Focused scores include one consolidated revision round; Extended scores include two. A revision round means one consolidated set of reasonable changes to an agreed creative direction. New cues, substantial rewrites, and changes caused by picture recuts are separately quoted. Rounds beyond the allowance cost $75 minimum or 10% of the commission fee per round, whichever is greater. Send each round as one collected set of notes: send everything at once rather than a note a day. See revisions FAQ and cancellation terms.

VDelivery - what lands in your inbox

Standard delivery is professional stereo WAV files of the agreed cues (24-bit, 48 kHz unless the project specifies otherwise), with a cue list and timings for the edit. Multitrack stems, alternate mixes, surround or immersive mastering, dialogue editing, final film mixing, comprehensive audio post-production and bespoke sound-design packages are not standard inclusions. Additional technical requirements may be considered individually after confirming feasibility. See deliverables FAQ.

Small trims inside existing cue usually absorbed as courtesy. Anything structural, scene reordered, sequence dropped, runtime extended, is recue and quoted before done. See picture lock FAQ.

VIRights, credit, and cue sheet filing

Each commission includes a standard project-use licence: use of the recorded score in the named production, its festival run and its own release, as set out in the written agreement, with copyright staying with the composer. Exclusivity, copyright assignment, soundtrack exploitation and expanded uses require a separate written agreement, and a copyright buyout is never included automatically. A signed agreement always beats this page. See licensing, rights and credits and who owns score FAQ.

End crawl: Music by Zazie Kanwar-Torge. Press and festival listings: Original score by Zazie Kanwar-Torge (Zazie Productions). Copyable wording at licensing page credit section. Production files cue sheet with PROs because it goes with picture delivery paperwork; composer supplies music side. See cue sheet FAQ.

The standard licence does not cover machine learning training on the music, stems or session data. No commissioned cue is composed by a generative model. See AI training FAQ.
