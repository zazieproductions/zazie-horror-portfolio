Zazie Kanwar-Torge is a psychological horror composer, sound designer, and experimental musician based in Asheville, North Carolina. He founded Zazie Productions LLC in 2022 to write original, dark, atmospheric scores for film, television, series, trailers, and games, specializing in psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, cosmic horror, techno thrillers, and dark science fiction.

Before moving into film, Kanwar-Torge spent years in the experimental music underground, releasing seven albums of dark ambient and experimental electronic music under the name Zazie Productions, most recently Anesthesia for the Signal Age (2026). He has scored nine selected productions, including the folk-horror series PEREGRINUS, the features UNSEEN and THE HAUNTED, and the 2025 horror short EXPIRE. Zazie Productions publishes scoring rates from $50 for micro-budget work to $8,000 for features, and is a Visual Container Winter 2024 Award Winner covered by BillboardWire, Limitless Magazine, and Grammy Weekly.

Standard · features, programs, profiles

Long bio (350 words)

Zazie Kanwar-Torge is a psychological horror composer, sound designer, and experimental musician based in Asheville, North Carolina, and the founder of Zazie Productions LLC, established in 2022.

His route into scoring runs counter to the usual one. Kanwar-Torge did not start with film; he started with the experimental music underground, where for years he built immersive audio worlds that blur the line between music, atmosphere, and perception. The results were released as seven albums under the name Zazie Productions — Vermiform, Stutter to Stammer, Spectral Ode to Synesthesia 2, Interference Archive 01010101, Constrained Capacity, To Halt Space Aadrift, and, most recently, Anesthesia for the Signal Age (2026) — distributed on Bandcamp, Spotify, and Apple Music. Limitless Magazine (June 2026) described Anesthesia for the Signal Age as turning "information overload into something strangely beautiful."

That experimental background is now the core of his horror approach. Rather than relying on jump scares or oversized orchestral stings, Kanwar-Torge's scores focus on what he calls the feeling that something isn't quite right. His techniques include distorted field recordings, manipulated textures, psychoacoustic illusions, unstable harmonies, bowed metal, waterphone, sub-frequency weight, hybrid orchestration, custom synths, and carefully controlled silence. In several of his scores, he notes, viewers cannot tell where the sound design ends and the score begins — which is intentional. "Most people think horror is about making audiences afraid," he told BillboardWire. "I'm more interested in making them question what they're hearing. The moment a viewer stops trusting their own perception, the film gains access to a much deeper kind of fear."

The film and television side of the studio has scored nine selected productions across psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural horror, and dark sci-fi, including the folk-horror series PEREGRINUS; the unseen-killer feature UNSEEN; THE HAUNTED, a Crystal Fox Films supernatural horror film; ECLIPSED, a dark sci-fi psychological horror film from VIERA Productions; CHOLERIC, an NYFA body-horror film; and the 2025 horror short EXPIRE. All scores are 100% original composition written to picture — no library packs, no stock cues.

Zazie Productions works remote-first from Asheville, North Carolina, scoring across several continents through video-call spotting sessions, with travel available for scoring stage, dub stage, or session. The studio publishes its rates openly: from $50 for micro-budget naming and student work, $2,500 for short films, $8,000 for features, and $4,500 for games, across four tiers (Lean, Standard, Signature, Orchestral) that include spotting, composition, stems, alternate mixes, cue sheet, and full rights to the score.

The studio is a Visual Container Winter 2024 Award Winner, is credited on IMDb, and has been covered by BillboardWire, Limitless Magazine, and Grammy Weekly.
