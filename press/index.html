IISixty-four compilation appearances - the underground record

Before the film work there was a decade of compilation appearances: harsh noise wall, musique concrete, dark ambient, field recording and witch house, on netlabels from Argali Records to Dittany of Crete, Owlripper, The Hills Are Dead, Camembert Électrique, Plataforma Recs, Gelombang Audiozine, Clan Analogue and GATES of HYPNOS. Discogs holds the release list and the netlabel pages hold the audio.
