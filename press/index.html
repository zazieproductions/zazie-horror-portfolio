250 words, with credits

Zazie Kanwar-Torge is a psychological horror composer, experimental producer and multi-instrumentalist, and the founder of Zazie Productions LLC, a boutique horror scoring studio in Asheville, North Carolina. The studio writes original dark, atmospheric and cinematic scores for psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, cosmic horror and dark science fiction, across film, television and games, and designs the unsettling sound the scores are built from — bowed metal, prepared piano, waterphone, contact-miked architecture, body-sourced foley and human voice used without language. Scores are written to picture, 100 per cent original composition, with no library music. Selected horror credits include EXPIRE (sound design), UNSEEN, PEREGRINUS, Phantom Requiem, ECLIPSED, THE HAUNTED, CHOLERIC, MIKE HAS A VISITOR and THE DARK AWAITS. Phantom Requiem (2024), written, directed, animated and scored by the studio, received a jury special mention at Pebbles Underground. The studio’s experimental work includes a musique concrète treatment of Erik Satie’s Gymnopédie No. 1 in tribute to John Cage’s Cheap Imitation, broadcast by Black Mountain College Radio, and a place in Canyon Cinema’s Films for Freedom online screening programme. The practice is documented in public reference documents — the Dread Grammar on scoring technique, a 67-term glossary of the vocabulary, a lineage map of psychological horror scoring, and a sound design method — so that directors, editors and supervisors can brief a rate of change rather than a genre. Commissions run from $50 micro-budget naming to $8,000 feature scores, with stems, M&E, cue sheets and defined rights at every tier.

IIIApproved quotations

These statements are cleared for republication with attribution to Zazie Kanwar-Torge, Zazie Productions LLC. Each is drawn from a published reference document, linked beside it, so the longer argument behind the quotation can be checked rather than paraphrased.

  • “Dread is not a mood. It is a rate of change: dread is a prediction failing slowly, fear is a prediction failing suddenly.” — The Dread Grammar
  • “A horror cue that sounds frightening in isolation is usually doing the wrong job.” — The Dread Grammar
  • “The score’s job is not to be scary. It is to remove the audience’s ability to predict the next few seconds, in a controlled way, for a controlled duration.” — The Dread Grammar
  • “Silence is the only material whose value increases the more carefully it is spent.” — The Dread Grammar
  • “Sound design in horror is subtraction. Record the physical world, then remove everything that identifies the sound, and keep what a listener can feel but cannot name.” — Sound design method
  • “Every horror score decays faster than any other kind, because the audience is learning the whole time.” — The Dread Grammar
