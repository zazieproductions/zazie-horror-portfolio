IEntry · Spectral Ode to Synesthesia

Impulse noise, stretched rubber, electromagnetic and underwater field recordings, taken apart with granular synthesis and placed around the head in a binaural mix. Best on headphones, in the dark.

Commissioned for the Metapsychosis open call and built specifically for headphone listening: the material is positioned around the listener rather than in front of them, so the piece changes shape if you turn your head to follow it.

For headphone listeners, sound-art programmers and anyone scoring a scene with no visual anchor.
