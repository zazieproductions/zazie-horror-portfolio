IEntry · Constrained Capacity

Dark ambient, industrial and noise rock in short, sharp cuts. Clan of the Rats, Vlad the Impaler, Drill & Plastic Bag Variation, Paranormal Woodland Incident of 03/03/22.

Where the later records stretch, this one truncates: the ideas arrive, hit and get out. The palette is guitar-adjacent and physical — drill, plastic, contact microphones — which is where the studio's body horror sound design later came from.

Useful for trailers and hard cuts that need aggression in under three minutes per track.
