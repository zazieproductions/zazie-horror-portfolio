IEntry · Motion-Sensor Skull Heads

Skull heads that stay quiet until someone walks past. Place them where the light is poor and let the sensor handle the timing.

A practical scare device rather than a recording: the trigger is the whole idea, because the effect depends on the visitor not knowing it is there. Used on set and in the studio, sold on with the rest of the prop shelf.

For filmmakers dressing a location, haunted-house builds and practical jokes with a horror budget.
