How to listen

Find the temperature first.

Start with “Needle in The Nerve” for psychological pressure, “Scraping Psychophonic Bed” for abrasive atmosphere, “Dark Ambient Soundscape No. 1” for a slow-moving space, or “Waterphone Shower Scene” for a more recognizable horror color. Then send the scene that needs that temperature.
