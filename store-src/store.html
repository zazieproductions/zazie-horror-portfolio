How buying works

Four ways off this page

Nothing here is a middleman storefront. Each card hands you to the platform that holds the file or the parcel, so licences, receipts and support stay where they belong.

  • 01

    Records, on Bandcamp

    Name your price above the minimum, then stream forever in the Bandcamp app and download in MP3, FLAC, WAV or whatever your archive prefers. Purchasable with gift cards; send-as-gift works too.

  • 02

    Libraries & tools, instantly

    itch.io and Gumroad deliver a download link the moment payment clears — WAV, MP4 or ZIP, royalty-free where stated. No DRM, no waiting for an email from me.

  • 03

    Objects ship from the listing

    The curiosities are listed on eBay under zazie_productions (100% positive, 24 ratings). Tracking, delivery estimates and buyer protection are handled there; delivery fees are shown on the card.

  • 04

    Or get it written for you

    If the sound you need isn't on the shelf — a theme, a tension bed, a creature pass — that work is commissioned rather than sold. Rates start at $50 for micro-budget and student films.
