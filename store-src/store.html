Four platforms, one shelf

This page is a catalogue, not a checkout. Each entry hands you to the platform that holds the file or the parcel, so receipts, licences and support stay in one place.

  • Records

    Bandcamp

    Name your price above the minimum. Stream in the Bandcamp app without limit and download in MP3, FLAC, WAV or whichever format your archive prefers. Gift cards accepted.

  • Libraries, tools, scores

    itch.io and Gumroad

    A download link arrives the moment payment clears: WAV, MP4, PDF or ZIP, royalty-free where stated. No DRM and no waiting for an email.

    Listed under zazie_productions, 100% positive across 24 ratings. Tracking, delivery estimates and buyer protection are handled by eBay. Delivery fees are shown on the card.
