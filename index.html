03 · Biography

The composer behind the atmosphere

Zazie Kanwar-Torge is an award-winning film composer, experimental producer, and multi-instrumentalist specializing in psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, and dark science fiction. Through Zazie Productions, he creates original film scores and immersive sonic atmospheres that explore dread, dissonance, isolation, and the uncanny.

Working at the intersection of cinematic scoring, musique concrète, and electroacoustic composition, his distinctive palette combines bowed metal, struck strings, experimental synthesis, and hybrid orchestration. His work has been featured at Ars Electronica 2026, performed in 20.4-channel spatial audio, and selected for international experimental music and film programs, including MUSLAB/Cero Records and EXiS in Seoul. Previously commissioned by the Black Mountain College Museum + Arts Center, he also develops original audio instruments and experimental sound libraries.

Drawing on a background in experimental film, sound art, and audiovisual composition, Zazie approaches each score as an extension of the film's psychological and emotional world, creating visceral, atmospheric music tailored to independent shorts, feature films, series, and horror video games.

What’s includedStandard delivery & optional add-ons

Included in every commission

  • Original music synchronized to picture for the cues agreed in writing.
  • Agreed musical scope and cue deliverables confirmed in writing before composition begins: spotting notes, cue list and tone references.
  • Defined consolidated revision rounds: one round for Individual Scene and Focused scores; two rounds for Extended and Feature/Experimental scores, as confirmed in the written quote.
  • Professional stereo WAV delivery (24-bit, 48 kHz unless your project specifies otherwise).
  • Cue titles and timings listed for your edit.

Specialist work, quoted on request

Discussed individually after a feasibility check. Not standard inclusions, and not part of a published estimate.

  • Multitrack stems by family
  • Alternate, tension-variant or clean mixes
  • Surround or immersive mastering (5.1, 7.1, Dolby Atmos)
  • Final film mix, dialogue editing or dub-stage supervision
  • Comprehensive audio post-production
  • Bespoke sound-design packages, foley and effects
  • Live musicians, soloists and session costs
  • Soundtrack album mastering and distribution
  • Adaptive or interactive music implementation for game engines
  • Trailer or teaser cut adaptation
