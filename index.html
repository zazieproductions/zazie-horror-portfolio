Read this before you “fix music later”

The costliest mistake in indie film isn’t the camera. It’s the night the room decides your movie is only “fine.”

People feel the score before they argue with the plot. Temp is rented feeling. Rented feeling does not get your film remembered.

01 · Problem

You’re directing two movies

The one you shot, and the one the temp track is rewriting. Every screening trains people to love music you don’t own and can’t clear.

02 · Stakes

Then the room goes cold

Festival silence. Supervisor shrug. Producer says “we’ll temp something.” What they won’t say out loud: it didn’t feel finished. It felt borrowed.

03 · Fix

Put original music on the cut

A dark, atmospheric, cinematic score written to your picture. Not a library pack. Not “we also do foley.” Music with a point of view so people lean in.

This is for you if

  • You care how the film feels in a silent room of strangers
  • You want original atmosphere, not “close enough” library cues
  • You’re a director, producer, or supervisor who values taste and speed
  • You’ll share picture, refs, and a real timeline
  • Even on a micro budget, you’ll name a real number ($50+)

This is not for you if

  • You want free “exposure” instead of a real engagement
  • You need production sound, foley, or full post FX (wrong shop)
  • You won’t lock picture or give notes, just “make it scary”
  • You’re shopping forty composers to reverse-auction the art
  • You want endless back-and-forth with no decisions
