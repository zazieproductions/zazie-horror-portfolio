03 · Approach

A distinct point of view, built for the story.

Every cue serves the picture. I plan the music with you. You get clear revision steps and delivery dates.

01

Psychological architecture

I shape each cue around the story. Slow beds build dread. Sharp sounds mark a turn or reveal. The music fits the cut, not a stock horror formula.

02

Dark atmospheric color

I blend strings, synths, and low sound to create a dark mood. Each sound has a story purpose. No stock "scary pack" cues.

03

Cinematic themes that stalk

A clear theme can carry a scene. It can also work in a trailer. The best themes stay with you after the film ends.

05 · Biography

The composer behind the atmosphere

Zazie Kanwar-Torge is an award-winning film composer and producer. He also plays several instruments. At Zazie Productions, he scores psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, and dark sci-fi. His music builds dread, tension, and isolation.

His palette mixes bowed metal, struck strings, synths, and orchestral sounds. His work appeared at Ars Electronica 2026. It was also presented in 20.4-channel spatial audio. MUSLAB/Cero Records and EXiS in Seoul have selected his work. The Black Mountain College Museum + Arts Center has commissioned him. He also builds audio instruments and sound libraries.

Zazie has a background in experimental film and sound art. He shapes each score around its story and emotional world. He writes for shorts, features, series, and horror games.
