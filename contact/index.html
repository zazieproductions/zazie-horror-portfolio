What to include

A short, specific brief beats a long pitch.

Tell Zazie Productions what the project is, what kind of music or sound work you need, and where the work currently stands.

  • Project type: short, feature, episode, game, teaser, or installation
  • Approximate runtime and the scenes that need attention
  • Picture-lock or delivery date
  • Budget range and rights/licensing requirements
  • Private screener, scene link, or written description of the tone
  • Whether the brief is score-only or intentionally hybrid with sound design

Do you compose original horror scores?

The portfolio is centered on original composition written to picture, including themes, beds, stingers, dark ambient work, and tension cues. The exact scope is agreed for each project.

Can you work on independent or micro-budget projects?

Independent and micro-budget work is represented in the portfolio. Send the budget, runtime, and delivery needs so the scope can be discussed honestly.

Do you provide Foley or production sound?

The site’s core offer is composition. Production sound, Foley, dialogue editing, and full post-production are separate disciplines and should be named in the inquiry if they are part of the need.
