IIWhat to send

Six things make a quote specific. Anything you cannot answer yet can be left blank.

  1. The format: short film, feature, series episode, game, trailer or installation.
  2. The scored minutes of music you need, and the cue count if you know it.
  3. The delivery date or festival deadline.
  4. The budget you can genuinely spend. A disclosed budget gets a useful answer rather than a guess.
  5. Two or three reference films or tracks for tone, and what each one does for the scene.
  6. A private link to a rough cut. The studio watches the cut before quoting, because runtime alone does not show how much music a film needs.

Unreleased material is treated as confidential. Tell me if you need a non-disclosure agreement before you send anything.

After review you receive a written, nonbinding quote that sets out the scope, the revision rounds and the licence. Nothing is booked until the scope, licence and payment terms are agreed in writing.
