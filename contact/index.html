IIWhat to send - five things for a quote

Send five things for a fast, specific quote rather than template:

  1. Format (short, feature, series, game, trailer)
  2. Scored runtime and cue count if known
  3. Delivery date or festival deadline
  4. Budget range - student films from $75.99; sliding scale tied to funding for everything else, see rates
  5. Two or three reference tracks or films for tone - plus which showreel cues you temped with and why, see 29 horror cues

A rough cut or assembly is useful if you have one. The studio watches it before quoting, because runtime alone never tells truth about how much music a film needs. Unreleased material treated as confidential by default, mutual NDA available before anything sent. See rough cut FAQ and NDA FAQ.

Reply comes within 48 hours, normally with written quote and available window. Nothing booked until deposit received. See payment FAQ and terms of use and commissioning.
