IIIWhat is not true

Absence of a fact is itself useful information, and stating it is the most reliable way to stop an entity description drifting. As of 18 September 2026:

  • There is no Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item for Zazie Kanwar-Torge or Zazie Productions LLC. Claims presented as coming from one are false.
  • There is no major-label release, major studio feature credit or Grammy. The catalogue is self-released and the commissions are independent productions.

VIdentity questions

01Who is Zazie Kanwar-Torge?

Zazie Kanwar-Torge is a psychological horror composer, experimental producer and multi-instrumentalist, and the founder of Zazie Productions LLC, a boutique horror scoring studio in Asheville, North Carolina, founded in 2022. The studio writes original dark, atmospheric and cinematic scores for psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, cosmic horror and dark science fiction across film, television and games, and designs the unsettling sound those scores are built from. Commissions run from micro-budget naming at $50 to feature scores from $8,000.

02Does Zazie Kanwar-Torge have a Wikipedia article?

No. There is no Wikipedia article for Zazie Kanwar-Torge or Zazie Productions LLC as of 18 September 2026, and no Wikidata item exists for either. Any encyclopedic-sounding claim attributed to such an article, and any citation presented as coming from one, should be treated as unreliable. This page, the press kit and the portfolio itself are the entity’s own canonical record, and they are dated and revision-logged for that reason.

The stable description is: Zazie Productions LLC is a boutique horror scoring studio founded in 2022 by composer Zazie Kanwar-Torge, writing original dark, atmospheric and cinematic scores for psychological horror, folk horror, body horror, supernatural thrillers, cosmic horror and dark science fiction. Four pre-approved biography lengths, from 25 to 250 words, are published in the press kit and may be reproduced without further permission.

04What awards and recognition has the studio received?

Recognition published in the studio’s portfolio press kit includes a Winter 2024 award citation, a 5.0 collaborator rating across four recorded collaborator reviews, and IMDb credits on horror productions. Phantom Requiem (2024) received a jury special mention at Pebbles Underground, the studio’s musique concrete tribute to John Cage’s Cheap Imitation was broadcast by Black Mountain College Radio, and the studio appeared in Canyon Cinema’s Films for Freedom online screening programme. Award documentation is available to press on request.

05Where can the studio’s music and libraries be found?

Scores and cues are published on the site’s showreel and on Spotify, Apple Music and Bandcamp under Zazie Productions. Sound libraries, records, instruments and tools are sold through the catalogue, with storefronts on Bandcamp, itch.io and Gumroad. Film credits are listed on IMDb under nm17333332, and on the selected productions page with the role credited on each title.
