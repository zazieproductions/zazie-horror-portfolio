IIIHow the score works

A supernatural horror teaser from Mike Fox at Crystal Fox Films — perception is reality. The score treats the haunting as a question of attention: atmospheric beds and haunted-house dread that never confirm the apparition for you.

Written to picture as original composition — no library music. The house method across these productions is composition-first: themes, harmony and texture are settled against the cut before anything is orchestrated, so the dread is structural rather than applied. The full method is documented on the scoring process page, and the vocabulary of the work — drone, bowed metal, struck strings, waterphone, custom synthesiser builds — on the composer page.

To hear the material outside a specific picture: the 29-cue showreel collects the psychological dread beds, tension stingers, dark ambience and body-horror cues that this production draws from.
