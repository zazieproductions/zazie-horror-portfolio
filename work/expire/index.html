IIHow the sound works

A 2025 horror short directed by Muhammad Abed Baryal. Zazie Kanwar-Torge is credited as sound designer, building the film’s body-horror textures and dread beds rather than a conventional underscore — the low end carries the picture where a melodic cue would explain too much.

This credit is sound design rather than a composed score, and the page says so plainly: the value here is the treatment of space and texture, not a theme. The full method is documented on the scoring process page, and the vocabulary of the work — drone, bowed metal, struck strings, waterphone, custom synthesiser builds — on the composer page.

To hear the material outside a specific picture: the 29-cue showreel collects the psychological dread beds, tension stingers, dark ambience and body-horror cues that this production draws from.
