From brief to picture lock

How I score horror.

The process is designed to protect the film’s emotional logic. A cue does not need to announce “horror”; it needs to know what the image is withholding, when the audience should anticipate a change, and what must remain unscored.

Six practical stages

Clear decisions, useful handoffs.

The exact schedule changes with the project. The sequence below gives directors, producers, and supervisors a shared vocabulary before any music is promised.

01 · Brief

Define the story pressure

Share the format, runtime, current cut, deadline, budget range, references, and the scenes where music must do real narrative work.

02 · Spot

Mark entrances and exits

Identify where the score begins, where silence has more authority, and which transitions need a bridge, a stinger, or a new texture.

03 · Palette

Choose the sound world

Decide between acoustic unease, hybrid orchestration, dark ambient space, industrial pulse, custom synthesis, or a deliberate combination.

04 · Compose

Build motifs and cues

Write to the picture rather than filling minutes. Motifs, tension beds, transitions, and release are tested against the edit.

05 · Refine

Use notes as story information

Revision is about dramatic intention: more distance, less certainty, a later reveal, a cleaner dialogue pocket, or a stronger turn.

06 · Deliver

Hand off a usable score

Depending on scope, delivery can include full mixes, stems, alternate mixes, variants, and picture-locked files. Rights and formats are agreed in writing.

What to send

A useful inquiry is specific.

A scene link or private screener, approximate runtime, picture-lock date, budget range, preferred references, and a sentence about the audience sensation are enough to begin. If the work is a hybrid score and sound-design brief, say so early.

Before you email

  • Is the cut available to spot?
  • Which scenes are priority?
  • Do you need score, sound design, or both?
  • What deliverables and rights are required?
  • Which external links can confirm the project?
