Scope bands what each package covers, minute by minute, and the feature-film exception

The whole-minute bands do not overlap. Individual Scene Scoring: Approximately 1–2 minutes of original music. Focused Short-Film Score: More than 2 and up to 5 scored minutes. IIIncluded in every commission

A commission is a composition service: original music written to your picture for the cues agreed in writing. Licence scope, copyright and soundtrack rights are separate and are described in section III. Anything not on this list is quoted on request.

Included in every commission

  • Original music composed to picture for the cues agreed in writing.
  • Creative scope confirmed in writing before composition begins: spotting notes, cue list and tone references.
  • Two defined revision rounds per cue, collected into one set of notes per round.
  • Professional stereo WAV delivery (24-bit, 48 kHz unless your project specifies otherwise).
  • Cue titles and timings listed for your edit.

Specialist work, quoted on request

Discussed individually after a feasibility check. Not standard inclusions, and not part of a published estimate.

  • Instrumental stems by family
  • Alternate, tension-variant or clean mixes
  • Surround or immersive mixes (5.1, 7.1, Dolby Atmos)
  • Final film mix, dialogue editing or dub-stage supervision
  • Sound design, foley and effects
  • Live musicians, soloists and session costs
  • Soundtrack album mastering and distribution
  • Adaptive or interactive music implementation for game engines
  • Trailer or teaser cut adaptation

IIILicence options

The base price covers a project licence. Wider rights change the price.

  • Non-exclusive project licence Base licenceUse in the named production, its festival run and its own release, as set out in the written quote.
  • Expanded distribution Adds 25% to the composition feeAdds broadcast, streaming and paid video-on-demand to the project licence.
  • Exclusive licence Adds 40% to the composition feeExclusive to the production for an agreed term and territory. The composer keeps the copyright.
  • Full buyout (copyright transfer) Quoted individuallyTransfer of copyright on payment. Quoted individually; not part of a published estimate.
  • Soundtrack album release Quoted individuallyCommercial release of the score as an album. Separate agreement; quoted individually.

Before you accept a quote

A full buyout (transfer of copyright) and a soundtrack album are quoted individually once the rights you need are discussed — never added to a published estimate, never folded into a low-priced package. Licence terms are set out in the written quote; the studio's standard licensing terms are being reviewed, so read them before you accept.

IVWhat moves the quote

Complexity, schedule, revisions, cue count and licence scope each move the figure. The estimator below applies the same rules as a written quote.

Scored music
Non-overlapping whole-minute bands: 1–2 minutes (Individual Scene Scoring); 3–5 minutes (Focused Short-Film Score); 6–12 minutes (Extended Short-Film Score). More than 12 scored minutes is quoted individually; scope labels are guides, not duration guarantees.
Starting fees
Published fees are starting points, not fixed-price caps. The written quote sets the agreed music scope and fee.
Cues
Two cues per scored minute are included. Each additional cue is $35 before the adjustments below.
Musical complexity
Focused palette no change; Layered hybrid +20%; Bespoke or experimental +40%.
Schedule
Three weeks or more no change; Two weeks +15%; Under ten days +30%.
Revisions
Two rounds per cue are included. Each additional round adds 6%, up to 3 rounds.
Licence
Non-exclusive project licence no change; Expanded distribution +25%; Exclusive licence +40%. A full buyout or soundtrack album is quoted individually.
Feature & experimental

The binding figure

An estimate is indicative and nonbinding. The written quote, issued after review of your cut, is the only binding figure.

VHow a commission starts

  1. Inquiry. Send a scoring inquiry with the scored minutes, the cue count if you know it, the delivery date, and a private link to a rough cut.
  2. Quote. Receive a written, nonbinding quote that sets out the scope, the revision rounds and the licence.
  3. Agreement. Scope and payment terms are confirmed in writing before composition begins.
  4. Delivery. Spotting, composition, two rounds of notes per cue, then the agreed WAV files.
