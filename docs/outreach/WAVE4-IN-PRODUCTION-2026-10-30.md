# Wave 4 — films in production, budgets attached, score not yet locked

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Status:** 7 drafts written, none sent.

Wave 3 targeted companies. This wave targets individual films that are
currently shooting or have just wrapped, announced with a budget or a
production grant, and whose crew lists show no composer. That is the window
where a score is actually still available — earlier the film is financing, later
it is temp-tracked.

## Why these titles and not a longer list

The rule applied here: a film had to clear **all four** filters before a draft
was written.

1. **Named as shooting or wrapped** in a trade or national film press report
   dated 2026, with a stated shoot window.
2. **Money stated** — a budget figure, a fund award, a tax credit, or a
   named financier. No "upcoming project" rows.
3. **No composer on the published crew list.** Several titles that otherwise
   fit were dropped for exactly this reason (see below).
4. **A reachable inbox** published by the production company itself, not a
   guessed address pattern.

## The copy

Same architecture as wave 3 — observation, identity, reel, one concrete
musical idea, a 15-minute call pegged to post, P.S. offering a single paid
cue — with one difference: because these are individual films rather than
companies, every email opens on the film's specific structural problem rather
than a house style.

The ideas, one per film:

| Film | The idea in the email |
|------|----------------------|
| A Body in the Woods | Score the corpse, not the witch: one low pulse, add one pitch per escalation, never a new cue |
| Picciriddi | One period song on a cheap radio, slowed and pitched down as the retaliation grows, melody unchanged |
| The White Week | Score at a child's ear height; each lie shifts the harmony a semitone and never returns |
| Effigy | One bowed drone that only changes in filter; the supernatural arrives by removing the filter, not by adding a sting |
| Almost You | Two voices from one sung phrase; the replica's version carries digital artefact, so the music tells the truth first |
| Salt & Bone | Build only from sounds that could exist in that world — tuned scrap, bone, breath, a piano missing two strings |
| Dilemma | A loop a few seconds too short, so the ear waits for a downbeat that never lands |

## Sending

Do not send these alongside wave 3. Wave 3 is company-level and evergreen;
this wave is perishable. Two of the seven are still shooting, so their value
decays within weeks.

Priority order, if sending selectively:

1. **A Body in the Woods** — closest genre match, in post now.
2. **Picciriddi** — just wrapped, Italian post moves fast.
3. **Effigy**, **The White Week** — both wrapped, both in post.
4. **Almost You**, **Salt & Bone** — still shooting; a reply now is early but
   not wrong, since a composer is normally hired at the end of principal.
5. **Dilemma** — shooting through late November; the email already says "once
   you're out of the shoot", so send it whenever.

Follow-up: one line at 12–14 days, then stop.

## Known limits

- Contact routes verified 2026-10-30 from first-party pages. Two of the seven
  are personal or general Gmail-class addresses published by the company
  (`Ian.nsenga@gmail.com`) or a group inbox (`hello@blazinggriffin.com`) — a
  lower-confidence route than a producer's own address, and it should be read
  that way.
- "No composer attached" is inferred from the published crew lists in the
  trade and national press coverage cited above. It is not confirmed by the
  productions, and a composer can be hired without a press mention. The email
  asks whether the score is locked rather than asserting it is open.
- Budget evidence is what was published. None of these numbers were privately
  confirmed.
- Nothing is sent, scheduled, or tracked. No attachments, no HTML, no pixel.
