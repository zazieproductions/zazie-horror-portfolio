Score-adjacent sound practice

Experimental sound design for audiovisual unease.

Struck strings, bowed metal, custom synth work, degraded signals, and noise-informed texture can sit between composition and sound design. Zazie Productions approaches that space with a composer’s ear and a clear boundary around what the brief actually needs.

Hear textural cuesScope sound and music

Material before mythology

Sound with a job in the scene.

Experimental does not have to mean opaque. A useful sound palette can identify its source, behavior, and dramatic role: a scrape that marks intrusion, a pulse that narrows attention, or a degraded tone that makes an image feel archival and unstable.

Acoustic disruption

Struck and bowed material

Metallic resonance, prepared-string color, and tactile gestures can give horror texture without relying on a generic “scary” preset.

Synthetic pressure

Custom synth and noise

Analog-style drift, filtering, distortion, and narrow-band pulses support industrial horror music and dark science-fiction atmospheres when the picture asks for them.

Picture-led restraint

Texture in service of edit

The sound bed should preserve dialogue, performance, and the film’s own rhythm. If the right move is silence, the palette should make that possible.

What this is—and is not

A precise brief prevents a blurry credit.

Good fit

  • Experimental horror score and soundscape work
  • Noise-influenced transitions, drones, and tension beds
  • Analog-horror or found-footage texture that is composed for the edit
  • A hybrid brief where the line between score and sound world is part of the concept

Clarify before booking

  • Production sound, dialogue editing, Foley, and full post-FX are separate disciplines
  • Rights, stems, source materials, and delivery formats belong in the scope
  • For a score-only project, start on the horror scoring page
  • For examples, use the on-picture archive and cue reel

Briefing prompt: send the scene, the intended audience sensation, the references you own or can share, and whether you need music, sound design, or a deliberately hybrid result.
