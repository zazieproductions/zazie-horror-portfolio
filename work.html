I Selected production records

The nine entries below state the verified role on each project; source links point to IMDb or the published project media.

Credit policy

The archive distinguishes verified project roles rather than treating every title as a Zazie composition credit. Each description names the specific contribution documented for the work, and no composing credit is attributed where a source does not support one.

    EXPIRE · Science-fiction short

    IMDb describes EXPIRE as a 2025 science-fiction short about AI toys in a ruined world. It credits Zazie Kanwar-Torge for sound design; Patricia Chaylade, Jorayo, and Brennan Mack are listed as composers.

    Genre: Science fiction · Verified role: Sound design · Source record

    IMDb describes UNSEEN as a 2026 horror-thriller feature and identifies Steve Merlo as its composer. This archive does not assign a composing credit to Zazie Kanwar-Torge.
