III By mood cluster

Browse by the kind of dread the cut needs — each cluster collects the cues that carry it, and opens the playable showreel on the portfolio home.

  • A

    Psychological orchestral cues

    The Beautiful Wrongness, Needle in The Nerve, Variations on a Vanishing Body, The Silence Began Crawling, Opaline Lament, The Room Forgets You, Time Shifting Into Daylight, Fractured Requiem. Slow-burn beds, dissociative harmony, orchestral dread shaped to cut.

  • B

    Body horror and textural

    The Fatal Overdose, Bone-Colored Air, Smell of Bleach. Visceral, textural, unsettling.

  • C

    Cosmic and supernatural horror

    Quarter-Eater Poltergeist, Obsidian Hall. Poltergeist otherworldliness, gothic dread architecture, and supernatural spaces.

  • D

    Ambient horror soundscapes

    Scraping Psychophonic Bed, Hidden Intentions / Serene Purgatory, Ominous Drone, Dark Ambient Soundscape No. 1, Subdued Drone / Sensory Deprivation, Dark Ambient Soundscape No. 2, Dissonant Decay : Soundscape Sampler, R'lyeh's Xenolith. Atmospheric horror beds for supernatural dread, sensory deprivation, and vast Lovecraftian soundscapes.

  • E

    Thriller and tension stingers

    Dissociative Amnesia, Brutalist Tension Stinger, Waterphone Shower Scene, Stinger Cello Cue (stingers); Timeless Retro Splurge, Deep Dystopian Synth Cue, Panic Threshold, Narrow Pulse, Earthpulse Protocol (thrillers). Pursuit, revelation, jump-scare.
