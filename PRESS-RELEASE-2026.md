# ASHEVILLE COMPOSER ZAZIE KANWAR-TORGE BUILDS A DEDICATED AUTHORITY FOR PSYCHOLOGICAL, FOLK, BODY AND ART-HOUSE HORROR SCORING

**ASHEVILLE, N.C. — [CONFIRM DATE]** — Zazie Productions, the scoring and sound-design studio of IMDb-credited composer Zazie Kanwar-Torge, today published a dedicated body of work for filmmakers working in psychological horror, folk horror, body horror and experimental art-house cinema: four subgenre-focused scoring resources, a 30-cue showreel organised by horror lane, and a supervisor-ready press room at horror.zazieproductions.com.

The launch formalises a practice already recognised outside the commercial film industry. Kanwar-Torge's spatial-audio work was featured at **Ars Electronica 2026** in 20.4-channel sound, and his scores and audiovisual works have been selected by **EXiS in Seoul (2025)** and **MUSLAB / Cero Records (2025)**, commissioned by the **Black Mountain College Museum + Arts Center**, and awarded a **Visual Container Winter 2024 Award**. His screen credits include composer roles on the sleep-paralysis short *MIKE HAS A VISITOR* and the paranormal series *THE DARK AWAITS*, co-composer credits on *ECLIPSED*, *THE HAUNTED* and the 2026 series *PEREGRINUS*, and sound design on the science-fiction short *EXPIRE* — each documented role-by-role, with source links, on the studio's website and IMDb (nm17333332).

Where most composer portfolios present a single generic reel, the new resources are organised the way directors and music supervisors actually shortlist: by subgenre. Each combines a definition of the lane, its scoring lineage and techniques, curated cues written to picture, and the studio's commissioning terms — including transparent published rates, deliverables, stems and cue-sheet support required for festival and distribution paperwork.

The studio's palette centres on performed waterphone, bowed metal, struck and prepared strings, musique concrète field recordings, granular and modular synthesis, and hybrid orchestration — acoustic sources recorded close and processed into textures that sit between score and sound design. ### 4.2 Wire services — honest assessment

Paid wires (PR Newswire, EIN Presswire, GlobeNewswire) guarantee distribution, not editorial coverage, and rarely move niche film-music journalists. For this studio's stage, a wire is worth considering only for (a) permanent searchable record of the announcement and (b) local-pickup aggregation. Spend the same budget first on a targeted, individually addressed pitch to 15–25 outlets above. If a wire is used, EIN Presswire-class pricing is sufficient; the premium tiers add no relevant audience here.

### 4.3 Timing and mechanics

1. Finalise and approve quote, date, fact sheet; confirm every URL resolves (especially `/press` and the four pillars).
2. Publish the release on `/press` first; this makes the studio's own site the primary source that later coverage links to and that AI engines cite.
3. Pitch 4–6 weeks ahead for monthlies/print; 1–2 weeks for daily blogs and horror outlets; Tuesday–Thursday mornings, no attachments over 5 MB (link the one-sheet and photos instead).
4. Follow up once, after five working days, with a single forwarded email — never a second new thread.
5. After coverage lands: add each mention to `/press` with outbound link, request the outlet link to `/composer` or the relevant pillar, and log the co-occurrence (outlet name + subgenre phrase + link) for the entity tracker.
