VCritical response - quotable, attributed

These lines are cleared for quotation in press and programme copy with the attribution shown. They come from David Finkelstein's review of Phantom Requiem (2024) in Lake Ivan Film Journal, published 24 March 2025.

  • “A nightmare, but an incredibly detailed, specific kind of nightmare.”

  • “The music, which is often structured into layers of repeating melodic motifs, starts off in a gothic mood, with strings, organ, and church bells.”

  • “The originality of the imagery is startling, and Kanwar-Torge has painstakingly assembled these layers of imagery and music into a gushingly assaultive onslaught with finesse and precision.”

  • “You will feel chill winds blowing through you for a long while after your five minutes with this powerful little film.”

Quotes may be shortened with an ellipsis but not reworded, and must carry the reviewer's name and the outlet. For longer excerpts, broadcast use or interview requests, write to the press desk — permission is granted quickly and in writing.

150 words · artist statements, panel introductions, sleeve notes

Zazie Kanwar-Torge is a psychological horror composer, multi-instrumentalist and producer working as Zazie Productions LLC from Asheville, North Carolina. Scores are written to picture rather than pulled from a library: slow-burn dread beds, dissociative harmony, sub-frequency weight and motifs built to survive the festival cut. The palette is hybrid — orchestral colour, bowed metal, struck strings, waterphone, granular synthesis, tape decay and custom synthesiser builds. Credits include EXPIRE, UNSEEN, PEREGRINUS, Phantom Requiem, ECLIPSED, THE HAUNTED, CHOLERIC and MIKE HAS A VISITOR, plus the original score for Are You Real? (2026), an official selection of the Golden Bloody Globes Film Festival in Los Angeles. Selected in 2026 from the international call for contributions, the multichannel electroacoustic work Slippage Beyond the Hyperlink was performed at the Ars Electronica Festival's Sonic Saturday, in the Sonic Lab of Anton Bruckner University, Linz. Recognition also includes the Visual Container Winter 2024 Award and selection as a BMC Radio Artist by the Black Mountain College Museum + Arts Center, Asheville FM and Make Noise.

XPress questions - answered before you ask

01Can I quote the review, the programme text or the artist bio?

Yes. Everything in sections V and VII is cleared for quotation with the attribution shown: name the reviewer and the outlet for review quotes, and cite Ars Electronica for programme text. If your edit is substantial, send the passage and a quick confirmation comes back in writing — usually within a day.

02Can I get a screener, audio excerpts or a high-resolution file?

Yes, on request. Phantom Requiem is public on YouTube; other excerpts, WAV previews of score material, stems for review, and larger image files are supplied directly for editorial use. Say what you need, the outlet, and your deadline.

03Will you give an interview about the score itself — instruments, process, the technical side?

That is the preferred interview. Expect specifics: spotting, temp scores, cue architecture, synth and instrument builds, deliverables and stems. Technical depth is welcome, including for audio and music-technology outlets.

04Do you speak on panels or appear at festivals?

Yes, subject to schedule. Remote participation is straightforward; travel is arranged for screenings, panels and scoring or dub stage sessions. Programmers, put the date and the theme in the first email and a real answer follows within 48 hours.

05How fast is the turnaround when I am on deadline?

Reply target is 48 hours, and same-day answers usually happen for press deadlines — mark the email PRESS DEADLINE with the time and time zone. Images, quotes and confirmations are sent as files or links, sized for use rather than as attachments that bounce.

06Who covers the horror scoring side of the business, the catalogue, or a festival booking?

All enquiries route through the same desk for now: zaziediya@gmail.com. Scoring and booking go through the scoring inquiry form; commercial catalogue and sound libraries live at the store.
