1The short version

  • No analytics package. No advertising pixel. No behavioural tracking of any kind.
  • No cookie is set by this site, which is why there is no consent banner to dismiss.
  • No account, no login, no newsletter, no marketing list.
  • The scoring form does not send your details to a server. It composes an email in your own mail application, and you press send.
  • Nothing about you is sold, rented or shared. There is nothing here to sell.
  • The only personal data that reaches the studio is what you choose to put in an email.
  • Video and audio come from third party hosts. Their players load only when you press play; one film sample’s cover image is fetched from its host when the page loads. The inventory is in section 6.

The rest of this notice exists because "we value your privacy" is not a statement anybody can check. What follows is checkable.

2Who is responsible

Zazie Productions LLC is the controller of the personal data described here. The studio is a single composer operation, so the person who reads your email is the person responsible for it.

Contact for any privacy matter, including a rights request: zaziediya@gmail.com. The studio is established in the United States and has no establishment in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. There is no separate data protection officer, because at this scale there is nobody else to appoint; requests are handled directly by the composer.

The site is hosted on an edge platform that serves the static files. That provider processes standard request metadata on the studio's behalf, described in section 3.

4Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. Not one session cookie, not one preference cookie, not one analytics cookie. There is nothing to accept and nothing to reject.

Two storage mechanisms are used on your device, and both are listed here because an honest notice says what it does rather than what sounds good:

  • One session storage key, zpBootShown, written by the opening sequence on the portfolio so it does not replay on every page in the same tab. It is deleted when you close the tab, it holds no personal data, and it never leaves your device.
  • A service worker cache, registered on load, which stores copies of pages, fonts and images in your browser so a repeat visit renders immediately and the site still opens offline. It is a local file cache, not a profile, and it can be cleared at any time from your browser settings or from section 5.

The privacy page reads your cookie jar, your storage keys and your cache names in order to show them to you. It writes none of them.

Your hosting provider or content delivery network may set its own cookie, for example a bot management cookie on your first request. That cookie is set by that provider under its own policy, not by this site, and the studio cannot read or remove it from here.

    7Embedded video and audio

    Film samples are embedded with the privacy enhancing youtube-nocookie.com host and no iframe exists in the page until you press play, so a visit that never plays anything never loads a player. The showreel audio is served from this domain.

    One image is the exception, and it is named rather than buried: the cover frame for the Eclipsed sample is requested from drive.google.com when the page loads, falling back to a self hosted image if that request fails. It is an image request and not a player, but it is a third party contact made without any action from you.

    One behaviour is worth stating precisely because it is easy to hide: hovering a film sample pre fetches the embed document from YouTube so that playback starts instantly. That is a network request to a third party made before you press play, and it is disclosed here rather than buried.

    If you would rather no third party were contacted at all, browse with images and media blocked, or use a browser that partitions or blocks third party requests. Every cue title, credit and description on this site is readable as text without loading a single embedded player.

    9Where data goes

    The studio, its mailbox and the site's hosting are in the United States. If you contact the studio from outside the US, your message is transferred there for the purpose of answering it, which is the transfer you asked for by writing.

    The studio does not onward transfer personal data to any other party, does not use it for marketing, and does not share it except where a project requires a session player or engineer who has signed the same confidentiality obligation as clause 14 of the terms.

    If you need the transfer mechanism that applies to a specific recipient, for example for your own compliance file, ask and the studio will answer in writing.

    10Your rights

    Rights are exercised by email to zaziediya@gmail.com. There is no form, no portal and no identity verification service, because there is no account to protect: the studio asks only for enough detail to find the correspondence you mean, and will ask a follow up question rather than request identification documents.

    In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

    • Access: a copy of what is held about you.
    • Rectification: correction of anything inaccurate.
    • Erasure: deletion, subject to the record keeping in section 8.
    • Restriction and objection: including objection to processing based on legitimate interest, which stops it unless there is a compelling reason to continue.
    • Portability: your correspondence and project files back in a usable format.
    • Withdrawal of consent, where consent was the basis, without affecting earlier processing.

    Requests are answered within one month. The studio has no establishment in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland and has not appointed a representative there, so requests go to the studio directly. That does not limit your right to complain to your own supervisory authority, and it does not affect any right you have under your local law.

    In California and other US states

    • Right to know what personal information is collected, the categories of source and the purpose. The tables above are that disclosure.
    • Right to delete, subject to the same record keeping exceptions.
    • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. The studio does not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of and no "do not sell" link is needed.
    • Right to non discrimination for exercising any of the above.
    • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.

    An authorised agent may make a request on your behalf with your written permission.

    Anywhere else

    The studio will apply the same standard to a request from any jurisdiction, whether or not a local law requires it. A request is answered on its merits, not on where it came from.

    11Security

    The site is served over TLS with security headers set at the edge, including X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, a restrictive Referrer-Policy and a permissions policy that disables camera, microphone and geolocation for every page. Project files are held on encrypted storage with access limited to the composer.

    Email is not end to end encrypted. If you need to send something genuinely sensitive, ask for an alternative and the studio will arrange one rather than have it cross the internet in the clear.

    If a breach ever affects personal data the studio holds, anyone affected will be told directly and without undue delay, and a supervisory authority will be notified where the law requires it.

    12Children

    This site is not directed at children, and the studio does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. The catalogue includes sound libraries and physical objects intended for adult buyers. If you believe a child has sent the studio personal data, say so and it will be deleted.

    13Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

    The site does not track, so a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal changes nothing here. Both are respected by default, in the sense that there is no tracking to switch off. If a third party host described in section 6 receives a signal from your browser, that host's handling of it is governed by its own policy.

    14Changes to this notice

    The effective date at the top of this page changes when the notice does. A change that expands what is collected, or that adds a third party, will be announced on the site before it takes effect rather than slipped in quietly.

    The inventory in section 6 is the part most likely to change, because it follows the site's build. If you spot a host there that this page does not name, that is a bug worth reporting.

    15Complaints

    Tell the studio first: zaziediya@gmail.com. Most complaints are a misunderstanding about what a page does, and those get fixed.

    If the answer is not good enough, you can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work: a national data protection authority in the EEA, the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, or your state attorney general in the United States. Nothing in this notice limits that right.
