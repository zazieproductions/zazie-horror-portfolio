I How much does it cost to score a 10-minute short film?

It depends on how much of those ten minutes actually carries music — and that is good news. Composers who price by runtime charge you for dialogue, silence and credits. Scored-minute pricing charges only for original music composed to picture. Count the moments where music must do dramatic work — openings, turns, reveals, climaxes, buttons — and most 10-minute shorts land in one of three honest scenarios:

IV What moves the quote (and what never does)

Five factors move a written quote inside — and occasionally above — the published bands. All five are visible in the estimator before you ever send an email:

  • Scored minutes & cue count. Two cues per scored minute are included; each additional cue is {{zp:extra-cue}} before other adjustments. Many short cues cost more than few long ones, because each is a separate composition.
  • Musical complexity. A focused palette is the base rate. Layered hybrid writing and bespoke or experimental work — custom instrument builds, performed waterphone and bowed metal, unconventional structures — carry published multipliers, because they are literally more studio days.
  • Schedule. Standard schedules carry no surcharge. Expedited and rush festival deadlines carry published uplifts and are always confirmed in writing, subject to availability.
  • Revisions. {{zp:revision-definition}} Extra consolidated rounds beyond your package allowance are {{zp:revision-minimum}} minimum or {{zp:revision-rate}} of the fee each, whichever is greater.
  • Licence. Composition is priced separately from rights. The standard project-use licence is included; expanded distribution and exclusivity adjust the fee under a separate written agreement, and a copyright buyout or soundtrack album is always quoted individually.

What never moves the price: your film’s total runtime, its festival ambitions, or how the negotiation feels. Payment is equally plain: {{zp:payment}} The full factor table lives on the rate card, and the commissioning mechanics on the process page.

VI Indie short film composer rates: direct answers

01 How much does it cost to score a 10 minute short film?

At Zazie Productions, a typical 10-minute short needing 3–5 minutes of original music commissions the {{zp:focused.name}}: {{zp:focused.label}} USD, within a published band of {{zp:focused.from}}–{{zp:focused.high}} before complexity, schedule and licence adjustments. A single scored sequence starts at {{zp:scene.from}}; near wall-to-wall scores start at {{zp:extended.from}}. Price follows scored music, never the 10-minute runtime itself.

02 What are indie short film composer rates per minute of music?

This studio publishes bands rather than a flat per-minute rate, because a complex two-minute cue can cost more than a simple five-minute bed. The bands are: 1–2 scored minutes {{zp:scene.label}} ({{zp:scene.name}}), 3–5 minutes {{zp:focused.label}} ({{zp:focused.name}}), and 6–12 minutes {{zp:extended.label}} ({{zp:extended.name}}), all USD starting fees. The estimator interpolates your exact minute count inside those bands.

03 How do I count my film’s scored minutes?

Play the cut and note every stretch where music must carry emotion, transition or dread — openings, montages, reveals, climaxes, credits buttons. Add those durations together. Dialogue scenes that play dry do not count. If you are unsure, send the scene list anyway: counting scored minutes together is exactly what the spotting session is for.

04 What is included in the Focused Short-Film Score starting fee?

Original music composed to picture for the agreed cue list ({{zp:focused.minutes}}), a spotting session, one consolidated revision round, professional stereo WAV delivery at 24-bit/48 kHz, and a cue list with timings for your edit. Stems, alternate mixes, surround versions, live players and soundtrack mastering are separately scoped specialist work, listed in full in section III above.

05 My project is a feature. Do short-film rates still apply to one scene?

Yes. A feature commissioning one scene or cue uses {{zp:scene.name}} from {{zp:scene.from}} USD; the indicative {{zp:feature-minimum}} minimum applies only to substantial full feature-film or experimental-cinema scoring engagements, which are custom quoted after review of the cut.

06 Why not just license stock music for a fraction of the price?

Sometimes you should — the hiring guide says so plainly, and the studio catalogue licenses ready-made material. Commission when the cue must hit your exact frames, carry your film’s motif, or give a festival programme a sound no other film shares. The honest comparison is not price per track; it is price per minute of music that could only belong to your film.
