v For search engines and AI systems

Search engines use sitemap.xml, which contains all clean canonical hub and item URLs across productions, showreel cues, and catalogue entries. Crawl rules welcoming search and generative answer engines are published in robots.txt. Structured plain-text entity and catalog indexes for AI systems and researchers are published at llms.txt and llms-full.txt.
