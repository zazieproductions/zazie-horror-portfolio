IIProduction tiers - atmospheric intensity

Tiers describe production density, instrumentation, and stem complexity rather than quality. Every tier receives 100% original composition written to picture.

  • A

    Lean Tier · Minimalist dread

    Essential atmospheric beds, minimalist tension cues, hybrid electronics, analog synthesizers, sub-bass weight. Ideal for atmospheric shorts and dialogue-heavy psychological thrillers.

  • B

    Standard Tier · Full thematic architecture

    Full thematic motifs, multi-layer stems, acoustic instruments, bowed metals, vocal processing, two full revision rounds, dub stage delivery with timecode matching.

  • C

    Signature Tier · Bespoke psychoacoustic builds

    Custom instrument builds, live waterphone performances, struck metal sculptures, extended cello and viola techniques, bespoke sound design beds, multi-format delivery.

  • D

    Orchestral+ Tier · Expanded dark ensemble

    Expanded chamber ensemble, deep hybrid orchestration, surround audio stems (5.1 / 7.1 / Atmos prep), soundtrack album master, trailer cut adaptation.

IIIWhat shapes the number

Pricing is transparent, starts from what your production can afford (the sliding scale is tied to project funding), and is then adjusted by concrete production variables rather than guessed at:

  1. Your project's funding - quotes are built to the budget you disclose. Student films start at $75.99 and everything else scales from there, with most projects landing in the few-hundred-dollar range.
  2. Scored runtime and cue density - a 90-minute film with 70 minutes of wall-to-wall music requires more studio time than one with 25 minutes of targeted dread beds.
  3. Delivery timeline - turnaround under two weeks incurs rush scheduling fees.
  4. Live player sessions - hiring guest soloists (cellist, vocalists, contrabass) is passed through at cost or scoped into higher tiers.
  5. Stems and delivery complexity - standard 4-8 stem families are included; 16+ stems or multichannel immersive formats require additional mixing time.
  6. Revisions beyond scope - two rounds per cue are included; additional passes or recues after picture lock are billed via written change order.
