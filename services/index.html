IScoring packages

Packages are chosen by scored music minutes: the length of finished original music you need, not the length of your film. A 90-minute film with three minutes of score is a Scene package. A 20-minute short with eight minutes of music is an Extended package.

IIIncluded in every commission

A commission is a composition service: original music written to your picture for the cues agreed in writing. Licence scope, copyright and soundtrack rights are separate and are described in section III. Anything not on this list is quoted on request.

Included in every commission

  • Original music composed to picture for the cues agreed in writing.
  • Agreed scope confirmed in writing before composition begins: spotting notes, cue list and tone references.
  • Revision rounds as stated for your package: one consolidated round (Individual Scene and Focused Short-Film), two (Extended Short-Film).
  • Professional stereo WAV delivery (24-bit, 48 kHz unless your project specifies otherwise).
  • Cue titles and timings listed for your edit.

Specialist work, quoted on request

Discussed individually after a feasibility check. Not standard inclusions, and not part of a published estimate.

  • Multitrack or instrumental stems
  • Alternate, tension-variant or clean mixes
  • Surround or immersive mixes (5.1, 7.1, Dolby Atmos)
  • Final film mix, dialogue editing or dub-stage supervision
  • Sound design, foley and effects
  • Live musicians, soloists and session costs
  • Soundtrack album mastering and distribution
  • Adaptive or interactive music implementation for game engines
  • Trailer or teaser cut adaptation
  • Comprehensive audio post-production

IIILicence options

The standard project licence is included in every published price: non-exclusive use of the delivered cues in the named production, including its festival run, its own release and its promotional use. Expanded distribution, exclusivity, a copyright assignment (buyout) and a soundtrack release each need a separate written agreement. They are quoted individually, and no amount for them is added to a package or an estimate.

  • Standard project licence Included in every published priceNon-exclusive use of the delivered cues in the named production: its festival run, its own release and its promotional use (trailers, clips and press), in the territories and for the term stated in the written quote. The composer keeps copyright and may show the work in a portfolio and showreel.
  • Expanded distribution Quoted separately in writingBroadcast, television, streaming platforms and paid video-on-demand, beyond the standard project licence. Separate written agreement; quoted individually.
  • Exclusivity Quoted separately in writingExclusive use of the cues in a defined field, term or territory. Separate written agreement; quoted individually.
  • Copyright assignment (buyout) Quoted separately in writingTransfer of copyright in the commissioned composition. Separate written agreement; quoted individually. Never included in a published price.
  • Soundtrack album or commercial release Quoted separately in writingRelease of the score as an album or other commercial audio product. Separate written agreement; quoted individually.

Licence terms are set out in the written quote. The studio's standard licensing terms are being reviewed; read them before you accept a quote.

IVWhat moves the quote

The estimator applies the same rules as a written quote. Complexity, schedule, revisions, cue count and licence scope each change the figure. An estimate is indicative and nonbinding. The written quote, issued after review of your cut, is the only binding figure.

Scored music
Priced by minutes of finished music, not film runtime. Individual Scene Scoring from $250 for up to 2 scored minutes; Focused Short-Film Scoring from $650 for up to 5 scored minutes; Extended Short-Film Scoring from $1,500 for up to 12 scored minutes. Scores over 12 scored minutes are quoted individually.
Cues
Individual Scene Scoring: up to 3; Focused Short-Film Scoring: up to 6; Extended Short-Film Scoring: up to 10. Cues above the allowance are composed and quoted individually.
Musical complexity
Focused palette no change; Layered hybrid adds 20%; Bespoke or experimental adds 40%.
Schedule
Standard schedule: no surcharge; Expedited: about 10–20 days: adds 15%; Rush: under 10 days: adds 30%.
Revisions
Individual Scene Scoring: one consolidated round; Focused Short-Film Scoring: one consolidated round; Extended Short-Film Scoring: two consolidated rounds. An additional round costs the greater of $75 or 10% of the commission fee.
Rights
The standard project licence is included. Exclusivity, expanded uses, copyright assignment and soundtrack release are quoted separately in writing.
Minimum fee
$250 (Individual Scene starting fee). Feature and experimental scoring: indicative minimum $2,500.

VHow a commission starts

  1. Send a scoring inquiry with the scored minutes, the cue count if you know it, the delivery date, and a private link to a rough cut.
  2. Receive a written, nonbinding quote that sets out the scope, the revision rounds and the licence.
  3. Agree the scope in writing. Payment terms are confirmed in writing before composition begins.
  4. Spotting, composition and the revision rounds in your package, then delivery of the agreed WAV files. The 50% deposit is due before work begins; the remaining 50% is due before release of the final approved deliverables.
