IIProduction tiers - atmospheric intensity

Choose production scale that fits score. Lean means fewer players, fewer stems, less mixing time than Orchestral+. All tiers include original composition, not library.

  • A

    Lean

    Essential dread architecture. Hybrid orchestration and custom synths, stereo mix, 4-6 stems, MP3 reference set, cue sheet. For micro and low-budget where every cue must count.

  • B

    Standard

    Full atmospheric narrative. Themes, beds, tension variants, 6-8 stems, alt mixes, stereo full mix, timecode-matched stems for dub stage.

  • C

    Signature

    Distinct point of view built for story. More thematic development, more alt mixes, deeper sound design integration, album-ready masters.

  • D

    Orchestral+

    More players, more stems, more mixing time. Live soloists or ensemble budgeted as separate line with players fees, raised at quote stage so writing is for real instruments.

IIIWhat makes number go up

Five things, in order they usually bite:

  1. Scored runtime and cue count, which is most of it.
  2. Production tier. Orchestral+ means more players, more stems, more mixing time than Lean.
  3. Compressed schedule. Delivery inside three weeks carries rush fee.
  4. Extra deliverables: surround or immersive mixes, album master, soundtrack release, source cues written for picture, sound design.
  5. Revisions beyond included rounds, or recue after picture lock.

See what moves price FAQ and terms of use and commissioning for cancellation, kill fee, and scope change handling. For sound design libraries, see catalogue: horror sound libraries.
