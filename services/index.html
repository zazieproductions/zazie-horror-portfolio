I Scoring packages

Choose a package by scored music minutes — the music composed for your film, not its runtime. Every published price is a starting fee in USD; the written quote confirms the scope and the figure.

Scope bands what each package covers, minute by minute, and the feature-film exception

The whole-minute bands do not overlap. Individual Scene Scoring: About 1–2 minutes of original music. Focused Short-Film Score: About 3–5 minutes of original music. Extended Short-Film Score: About 6–12 minutes of original music. A feature-film client commissioning just one scene or cue may use Individual Scene Scoring; the indicative feature minimum applies only to substantial full-project scores. Published prices are starting fees, not fixed-price caps or a guarantee of a specific duration.

II Included in every commission

A commission is a composition service: original music written to your picture for the cues agreed in writing. Licence scope, copyright and soundtrack rights are separate and are described in section III. Anything not on this list is quoted on request.

Included in every commission

  • Original music for the cues listed in your written agreement.
  • Before work starts, we agree on the music scope and deliverables. This includes spotting notes, the cue list, and tone references.
  • Revision rounds depend on your package. Individual Scene and Focused scores include one round. Extended and Feature/Experimental scores include two. Your quote lists the allowance.
  • Stereo WAV files in 24-bit, 48 kHz. Tell me if your project needs another format.
  • Cue titles and timings listed for your edit.

Specialist work, quoted on request

Ask me about these services. They are not standard inclusions. They are not part of a published estimate.

  • Multitrack stems by family
  • Alternate, tension-variant or clean mixes
  • Surround or immersive mastering (5.1, 7.1, Dolby Atmos)
  • Final film mix, dialogue editing or dub-stage supervision
  • Comprehensive audio post-production
  • Bespoke sound-design packages, foley and effects
  • Live musicians, soloists and session costs
  • Soundtrack album mastering and distribution
  • Adaptive or interactive music implementation for game engines
  • Trailer or teaser cut adaptation

III Licence options

The commercial pricing model uses a standard project-use licence for the named production, its festival run and its own release, with copyright staying with the composer. Expanded distribution, exclusivity, copyright assignment and soundtrack exploitation require a separate written agreement. The operative licensing and terms pages still describe a buyout as the default, so applicable rights must be confirmed in the written quote and signed agreement while those documents are reconciled.

  • Standard project-use licence Base licenceUse the score in the named production, its festival run, and its own release. Your written agreement sets the terms. The composer keeps the copyright.
  • Expanded distribution Adds 25% to the composition fee under a separate written agreementAdds broadcast, streaming, and paid video-on-demand use. Requires a separate written agreement.
  • Exclusive licence Adds 40% to the composition fee under a separate written agreementLimits the music to your production for an agreed time and region. Requires a separate written agreement. The composer keeps the copyright.
  • Copyright assignment (buyout) Quoted individually under a separate written agreementTransfers copyright after payment. Quoted separately and never included by default.
  • Soundtrack album release Quoted individually under a separate written agreementFor a commercial soundtrack album. Requires a separate agreement and quote.

Before you accept a quote

A full buyout (transfer of copyright) and a soundtrack album are quoted individually once the rights you need are discussed — never added to a published estimate, never folded into a low-priced package. Licence terms are set out in the written quote; the studio's standard licensing terms are being reviewed, so read them before you accept.

IV What moves the quote

Complexity, schedule, revisions, cue count and licence scope each move the figure. The estimator below applies the same rules as a written quote.

Starting fee, not a ceiling
Each package publishes a starting fee, not a guaranteed maximum amount of music. A complex two-minute composition may require a higher quote than a simple atmospheric cue of the same length.
Scored music
Non-overlapping whole-minute bands: 1–2 minutes (Individual Scene Scoring); 3–5 minutes (Focused Short-Film Score); 6–12 minutes (Extended Short-Film Score). More than 12 scored minutes, multi-part or expanded-scope work is quoted individually; scope labels are guides, not duration guarantees.
Cues
Two cues per scored minute are included. Each additional cue is $50 before the adjustments below.
Musical complexity
Focused palette no change; Layered hybrid +20%; Bespoke or experimental +40%.
Schedule
Standard schedule no change; Expedited: about 10–20 days +15%; Rush: under 10 days +30%. Rush is subject to availability.
Revisions
One consolidated round is included for Individual Scene and Focused scores; two rounds for Extended and Feature/Experimental scores, as confirmed in the written scope. Each additional round is $75 minimum or 10% of the commission fee, whichever is greater, up to 3 rounds. A revision round means one consolidated set of reasonable changes to an agreed creative direction. New cues, substantial rewrites, and changes caused by picture recuts are separately quoted.
Licence
Standard project-use licence no change; Expanded distribution +25%; Exclusive licence +40%. Exclusivity, copyright assignment, soundtrack exploitation and expanded uses require a separate written agreement. A copyright buyout or soundtrack album is quoted individually.
Feature & experimental
Custom quotation. The indicative minimum of $2,500 applies only to substantial feature-film or experimental-cinema scoring engagements; a single scene/cue for a feature may use Individual Scene Scoring.
Individual scene minimum
$250 starting fee.
Payment
50% deposit before work begins. Remaining 50% due before release of final approved deliverables, subject to the written agreement. Applies to new commissions only; existing contracts are unchanged.

V How a commission starts

  1. Inquiry. Send a scoring inquiry with the scored minutes, the cue count if you know it, the delivery date, and a private link to a rough cut.
  2. Quote. Receive a written, nonbinding quote that sets out the scope, the revision rounds and the licence.
  3. Agreement. Scope and payment terms are confirmed in writing before composition begins.
  4. Delivery. Spotting, composition, the package's consolidated revision allowance, then the agreed WAV cues and cue list.
