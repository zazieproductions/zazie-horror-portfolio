IScoring packages

Packages are chosen by scored music minutes: the length of finished original music you need, not the length of your film. A 90-minute film with two minutes of score is a Scene package. A 20-minute short with eight minutes of music is an Extended package. Packages reflect scope as well as minutes: a single cue, a spotted short with its own motifs, and a developed thematic score are different commissions.

IIIncluded in every commission

A commission is a composition service: original music written to your picture for the cues agreed in writing. Licence scope, copyright and soundtrack rights are separate and are described in section III. Anything not on this list is quoted on request.

Included in every commission

  • Original music synchronized to picture for the cues agreed in writing.
  • Agreed musical scope and cue deliverables confirmed in writing before composition begins: spotting notes, cue list and tone references.
  • Defined consolidated revision rounds: one round for Individual Scene and Focused scores, two rounds for Extended scores.
  • Professional stereo WAV delivery (24-bit, 48 kHz unless your project specifies otherwise).
  • Cue titles and timings listed for your edit.

Specialist work, quoted on request

Discussed individually after a feasibility check. Not standard inclusions, and not part of a published estimate.

  • Multitrack stems by family
  • Alternate, tension-variant or clean mixes
  • Surround or immersive mastering (5.1, 7.1, Dolby Atmos)
  • Final film mix, dialogue editing or dub-stage supervision
  • Comprehensive audio post-production
  • Bespoke sound-design packages, foley and effects
  • Live musicians, soloists and session costs
  • Soundtrack album mastering and distribution
  • Adaptive or interactive music implementation for game engines
  • Trailer or teaser cut adaptation

IIILicence options

The base price covers a standard project-use licence. Wider rights change the price. Exclusivity, copyright assignment, soundtrack exploitation and expanded uses require a separate written agreement. A copyright buyout and a soundtrack album are quoted individually once the rights you need are discussed. Neither is added to a published estimate, and neither is included automatically in a low-priced package.

  • Standard project-use licence Base licenceUse of the recorded score in the named production, its festival run and its own release, as set out in the written agreement. Copyright stays with the composer.
  • Expanded distribution Adds 25% to the composition fee (separate written agreement)Adds broadcast, streaming and paid video-on-demand to the project licence, by separate written agreement.
  • Exclusive licence Adds 40% to the composition fee (separate written agreement)Exclusive to the production for an agreed term and territory, by separate written agreement. The composer keeps the copyright.
  • Copyright assignment (buyout) Quoted individually under a separate written agreementTransfer of copyright on payment. Quoted individually under a separate written agreement; never included automatically.

Licence terms are set out in the written quote. The studio's standard licensing terms are being reviewed; read them before you accept a quote.

IVWhat moves the quote

The estimator applies the same rules as a written quote. Complexity, schedule, revisions, cue count and licence scope each change the figure. Each estimate is a preliminary estimate — final quotation subject to creative scope and written agreement. The written quote, issued after review of your cut, is the only binding figure.

Starting fee, not a ceiling
Each package publishes a starting fee, not a guaranteed maximum amount of music. A complex two-minute composition may require a higher quote than a simple atmospheric cue of the same length.
Scored music
Priced by minutes of finished music. Packages cover up to 12 scored minutes; longer work is quoted individually. Additional composition is quoted individually from scored minutes, cue count and creative complexity.
Cues
Two cues per scored minute are included. Each additional cue is $50 before the adjustments below.
Musical complexity
Focused palette no change; Layered hybrid +20%; Bespoke or experimental +40%.
Schedule
Standard schedule no change; Expedited: about 10–20 days +15%; Rush: under 10 days +30%. Rush is subject to availability.
Revisions
One consolidated round is included for Individual Scene and Focused scores; two rounds for Extended scores. Each additional round is $75 minimum or 10% of the commission fee, whichever is greater, up to 3 rounds. A revision round means one consolidated set of reasonable changes to an agreed creative direction. New cues, substantial rewrites, and changes caused by picture recuts are separately quoted.
Licence
Standard project-use licence no change; Expanded distribution +25%; Exclusive licence +40%. Exclusivity, copyright assignment, soundtrack exploitation and expanded uses require a separate written agreement. A copyright buyout or soundtrack album is quoted individually.
Minimum fee
$250.
Payment
50% deposit before work begins. Remaining 50% due before release of final approved deliverables, subject to the written agreement.

VHow a commission starts

  1. Send a scoring inquiry with the scored minutes, the cue count if you know it, the delivery date, and a private link to a rough cut.
  2. Receive a written quote that sets out the scope, the revision rounds and the licence. It is a preliminary estimate — final quotation subject to creative scope and written agreement.
  3. Agree the scope in writing. 50% deposit before work begins. Remaining 50% due before release of final approved deliverables, subject to the written agreement.
  4. Spotting, composition, the included consolidated revision rounds, then delivery of the agreed WAV files.
